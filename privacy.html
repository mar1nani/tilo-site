Privacy Policy

Last updated: 26 November 2026. Contact: tiloplayer.support@gmail.com

What Tilo Player is

Tilo Player is a media player for playlists you already have the right to use. The app contains no channels, films or series and does not provide any content.

What the app stores on your device

Your playlist address and login, your library list (titles, categories, artwork links), your favourites, your watch progress and your settings. This data stays on your device. We do not receive it, except what the optional “send from your phone” service described below passes to your device.

What the app sends over the internet

To the playlist provider you entered: requests for your list and for the streams you choose to play. We do not operate those servers and do not see that traffic. The app also contacts our server for the services described below.

Free trial and activation (Android app)

This applies to the Android app (from Google Play, version 1.0.7 or later, or the trial file from our website). The Samsung TV version does not do this.

The app starts with a free trial of 7 days. To manage the trial and activation, the app contacts our server when it opens and regularly while it is used, and sends:

  • the Device ID (a code derived from your device, not your name and not its hardware address);
  • the device type (phone, tablet or TV) and model.

From the internet connection used for that request, our hosting provider gives us an approximate location (country and city). We do not store your IP address with it. We keep the Device ID, trial and activation dates, device type and model and approximate location, together with a label we may give the device, so that we can manage activations, prevent a trial from being restarted and help you if you contact us. Our administrator can see this record. An authorised reseller to whom you give your Device ID can look up that device’s record in order to activate it. This record is kept until you ask us to delete it.

Optional “send from your phone” service

The app shows a Device ID (a code derived from your device, not your name). While the welcome screen or the “send from your phone” screen is open, the app sends this Device ID to our server to check whether a playlist is waiting for it.

If you choose to use our setup page, you enter that Device ID and your playlist link, file or login. We keep them only to hand them to your device: they are deleted as soon as your device collects them, and in any case after 10 minutes. We do not read or keep the content of your playlist.

To prevent abuse, our servers briefly keep technical counters linked to your IP address (at most one hour). Our services are hosted by Vercel and Upstash, which process this data on our behalf. You can use the app without the “send from your phone” service by typing your playlist details on the device.

What we do not do

No accounts, no advertising, no advertising ID, no analytics tools, no sale of your data.

Deleting your data

In the app, go to Settings, then "Delete all my data". This removes everything the app stored on your device. Uninstalling the app also removes it. Neither removes the trial and activation record kept on our server: to have it deleted, write to the contact address above with your Device ID.

Children

Tilo Player is a general-purpose player and is not directed to children.

Your rights

You can ask to see, correct or delete the trial and activation record linked to your Device ID, or ask any privacy question, by writing to the contact address above. Playlist details sent through the setup page are deleted within minutes, so there is normally nothing of them left to access.

Changes

We will update this page if the app changes how it handles data.
