Privacy Policy

Last updated: 23 November 2026. Contact: tiloplayer.support@gmail.com

What Tilo Player is

Tilo Player is a media player for playlists you already have the right to use. The app contains no channels, films or series and does not provide any content.

What the app stores on your TV

Your playlist address and login, your library list (titles, categories, artwork links), your favourites, your watch progress and your settings. This data stays on your TV. Apart from the optional service described below, we do not receive it.

What the app sends over the internet

Only to the playlist provider you entered: requests for your list and for the streams you choose to play. We do not operate those servers and do not see that traffic.

Optional “send from your phone” service

The app shows a Device ID (a code derived from your device, not your name). While the “send from your phone” screen is open, the app sends this Device ID to our server to check whether a playlist is waiting for it.

If you choose to use our setup page, you enter that Device ID and your playlist link, file or login. We keep them only to hand them to your device: they are deleted as soon as your device collects them, and in any case after 10 minutes. We do not read or keep the content of your playlist.

To prevent abuse, our servers briefly keep technical counters linked to your IP address (at most one hour). This service is hosted by Vercel and Upstash, which process this data on our behalf. You can use the app without this service by typing your playlist details on the device.

What we do not do

No accounts, no advertising, no analytics, no tracking, no sale or sharing of your data.

Deleting your data

In the app, go to Settings, then "Delete all my data". This removes everything the app stored. Uninstalling the app also removes it.

Children

Tilo Player is a general-purpose player and is not directed to children.

Your rights

Because we keep almost no data about you (the optional service above is deleted within minutes), there is normally nothing to access, correct or delete on our side. If you have a privacy question or request, write to the contact address above.

Changes

We will update this page if the app changes how it handles data.
