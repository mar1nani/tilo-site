Support

Write to tiloplayer.support@gmail.com. Please tell us your TV model and what happened.

Getting started

  1. Open Tilo Player and go to Settings, then Playlist.
  2. Enter your playlist details. The app comes empty, because it includes no content.
  3. Wait for the library to load, then use Home, Movies, Series, Live TV and Search.

Common questions

Where are the channels and movies? Tilo Player is only a player. Add a playlist from a source you are allowed to use.

A stream will not play. Check your internet connection and that your playlist account is active. The message on screen explains what failed.

How do I delete my data? Settings, then "Delete all my data".

Is my data sent to you? Your library stays on your device. The Android app sends a Device ID and device model to manage its free trial and activation. See the Privacy Policy.
